Why are we doing this

Everyone should have an domain. And when I made my own small homeserver, I was frustrated as there was no good, modern and easy to use free domain system. All that I saw where only behind invite-only codes, long closed or not working. So, I decided to m,ake my own Free domain service.


Whats the catch?

Well, there is... None?

No seriusly. Just try it out! You only need to register yourself an account, where you just need an Authenticator app and thats it. No personal information is needed. Once registering, you will get an Log - In code, and you will setup Authy or any other alternative authenticator app. And then you are ready to go!
